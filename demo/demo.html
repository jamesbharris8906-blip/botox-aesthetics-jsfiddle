Botox

Botox is the treatment most men start with, and it is the simplest. A few tiny injections, a few minutes, and you are done. The needles are very fine, and most people feel a small pinch that lasts a second. Afterward, avoid lying down or rubbing the area for a few hours, and skip heavy exercise that day. The lines soften over the next several days, and the result peaks at about two weeks. If you have a big presentation, a job interview, or a family photo coming up, book two to three weeks before, so the effect is at its best on the day.

Microneedling

Microneedling is a good option if your skin has rough texture, acne scars, or large pores. Tiny needles prompt the skin to make new collagen, which smooths the texture over a few weeks. There is redness for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, so plan it around your calendar. A series of treatments usually gives the best result, and the team will be honest about how many you are likely to need. It is not a quick fix, but it is a real one, and the result lasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Botox just for women?

No. Men make up a growing share of Botox clients, and the treatment is the same. The dose may be a little different, because men's facial muscles are often stronger, and the team will adjust it for you.

Will my coworkers know I had Botox?

Not if it is done well. A conservative dose softens the lines while letting you move your face normally. Most people will think you look rested, not that you had a treatment.

How long does a first visit take?

A consultation takes about half an hour, and a Botox treatment takes a few minutes after that. A Diamond Glow Facial takes about an hour. You can fit most visits into a lunch break.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel a small pinch that lasts a second. The needles are very fine. If you are nervous, tell the team, and they will go slowly and talk you through each step.

How long does Botox last for men?

About three to four months for most people, sometimes a little less if your muscles are strong. The team will tell you when to book your next visit, based on how your face responds.

Can I exercise after Botox?

Wait until the next day for heavy exercise. Avoid lying down or rubbing the treated area for a few hours after the appointment, and follow the aftercare advice the team gives you.

What should I do first if I am not sure?

Book a consultation. The team will look at your skin, listen to what bothers you, and recommend the treatment that fits, with a written price. If nothing is needed yet, they will say so.
